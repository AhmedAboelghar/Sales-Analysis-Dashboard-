# 📊 Sales Dashboard 2026 - Power BI Analytics

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce and retail sales trends, product performance, customer metrics, and regional distributions.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides business stakeholders with clear, data-driven insights into sales trajectories, top-performing product categories, and overall revenue metrics. It helps monitor performance trends over time and evaluate product category market shares.

---

## 🔑 Key Metrics & Highlights (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $68.52K
* **Total Orders Processed:** 71 Orders
* **Unique Customers:** 12 Customers
* **Top Product Category:** Laptop (Accounting for **48.8%** of total sales / $33.44K)
* **Top Selling Product:** Dell XPS 15 ($13.9K in total sales)

---

## 📈 Features & Visualizations
1. **Executive KPI Cards:** High-level summary of total sales, customer volume, and total order count.
2. **Sales Trend Analysis (Time Series Line Chart):** Tracks sales performance by Year and Quarter from Q1 2024 through Q1 2026.
3. **Category Share Breakdown (Donut Chart):** Visualizes sales distribution across categories (*Laptop, Smartphone, Tablet, Smart Home, Accessory*).
4. **Top Products Analysis (Horizontal Bar Chart):** Ranks products by revenue contribution (e.g., *Dell XPS 15, HP Spectre x360, iPad Air 6, Galaxy S24*).
5. **Interactive Filtering (Slicers):**
   * **Date Range Filter:** Dynamic time slice selection (`01/04/2024` to `03/29/2026`).
   * **Geographic Filter:** Slicing by country (*China, Germany, India, United States*).
   * **Category Filter:** Filtering by product category.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Dashboard design, data modeling, and visualization.
* **Power Query:** Data transformation, cleaning, and preparation.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Measures for aggregation and KPI tracking.
